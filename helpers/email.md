# Email helper

## Look
- Emails from the last 24 hours (or since the last briefing)
- Only things that need action or matter: appointments, deadlines, payments, replies Darren owes

## Skip
- Newsletters, adverts, receipts with nothing to do

## Bring back (short list, one line each)
- Who, what they want, any date/deadline
- Don't recap the whole email. Never reply or send anything.
