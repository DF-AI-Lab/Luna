Luna

Task card revamp: five options. Each card has only a name, a short description, a line from Luna, one way to finish, and a mic that opens Ask Luna about that card. Tap things to try them.

Option 1 – Moon check

One big circle to tick, nothing else to decide. Remind or drop by telling Luna. No OK button.

Option 2 – Slide to finish

Slide the thumb across to complete: satisfying, and hard to do by accident. No OK button.

Option 3 – Checklist rows

The calmest and most compact: one panel, a tick box per row, a quiet mic. No OK button.

Option 4 – Progress ring

A ring fills as the day gets done. One bold Done button; OK and Remind merge into a quiet "Later".

Option 5 – One at a time

Only the next task is shown in full, the rest wait quietly. "Skip" is the subtle OK.
