Your phone

The screen

The Luna icon on your home screen. Tap Done, Later, Skip or reply to a card.

Replies wait in the page's own little store until the next run picks them up. No Claude usage here.

next run copies replies into log/

Orbit

Learns from log/

Your own app reads log/ from the repo. Each line pairs a card with your answer, so it can learn your habits without AI.

Actions like "yes, add it to calendar" happen on the next run, or straight away if you open a chat and say go.
